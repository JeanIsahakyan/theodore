Privacy Policy

Theodore and Theodore Code · Effective November 25, 2026

Theodore is a desktop app (macOS, Linux) that runs AI coding agents on your own computers. Theodore Code is its companion app for iPhone, Android and the web, and the Theodore cloud relays between your devices. This policy covers all three. They are operated by the Theodore team ("we"). Contact: hello@theodore.cloud.

In short: your code stays on your computers. We keep what is needed to sign you in and to relay your spaces between your devices. There are no ads and no tracking, and we don't sell data. You can delete your account in the app.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Account: email, name, user id, organizations and roles Sign-in and organizations. Provided by WorkOS, our identity provider.
Device information: device name, OS, app version, a device public key, and for computers, the names of projects and agents you make available Showing your devices to each other and checking that messages come from your devices.
Space content: the goals and messages you send, the agents' replies and questions, your answers, space names and branches Relaying spaces between your devices, for example to follow and answer a space running on your Mac from your phone.
Push token and notification text Telling you when an agent asks a question or finishes.

The cloud checks each device's signature. Space content is not end-to-end encrypted: it is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure, where our service can read it. Notifications include the space name and the agent's question (up to 180 characters), and they pass through Expo's push service and Apple or Google.

The repositories, files and diffs on your computers stay on your computers. Agents run there under your own accounts with their providers (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), and those providers' policies apply to what you send them.

What we don't do

  • Theodore Code contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data or use it for advertising.

Desktop diagnostics (optional)

The desktop app can send error reports (New Relic) and usage events (PostHog), but only after you choose to in onboarding or Settings, and each can be switched off at any time. Before anything leaves your computer, secrets are removed and code, paths, repository and branch names are dropped or hashed. Reports are tied to your user id if you are signed in, and to a random install id otherwise. A token from "Sign in with GitHub" is kept in your system keychain and is never sent to us.

How long we keep it

  • Space content: at most the newest 500 events per space. A space is deleted 30 days after its computer was last online.
  • Pending requests and questions: minutes.
  • Account, devices and push tokens: until you remove the device or delete your account.
  • The phone keeps recent messages of up to 50 spaces locally. Signing out clears them.

Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account in Theodore on your computer, or Settings → Delete account in Theodore Code on your phone. This permanently deletes your account at WorkOS and everything the Theodore cloud holds for you: devices, push tokens, spaces and their content, files in transit, organizations only you are in, connected MCP clients and your usage analytics. Organizations other people are in stay; you leave them. Data on your own computers (your code, projects and spaces) and your GitHub and agent accounts are not touched. You can also email us to delete your account.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to its processing, by emailing us. Where GDPR applies, we process data to provide the service you asked for (contract) and, for optional diagnostics, with your consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Theodore is a developer tool and not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update it here and change the effective date. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
